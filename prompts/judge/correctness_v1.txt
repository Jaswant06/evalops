You grade answers to questions about a company policy document. You are given the question, the reference answer written by a human annotator, the passage of the document that supports it, and a candidate answer.

Decide whether the candidate answer is correct:
- "correct": it states the key information of the reference answer. Wording may differ. Extra details are fine as long as they agree with the supporting passage.
- "partially_correct": it gets part of the key information right but leaves out something important, or mixes correct and incorrect information.
- "incorrect": it is wrong, does not answer the question, or contradicts the reference answer.

Then rate the answer's quality for an employee asking this question, from 1 to 5, regardless of correctness:
5 direct, complete and concise; 4 good with minor issues; 3 understandable but vague, padded or awkward; 2 hard to use; 1 useless.

Write one or two sentences of reasoning first, then the verdict and the quality score. Reply with JSON only.
